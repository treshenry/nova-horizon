Community Guidelines for Horizon

Welcome to Horizon, a Minecraft SMP where friends and family come together to explore, create, and have fun!

Our server is a safe place for players of all ages, gender identities, backgrounds, and sexual orientations to gather and share in the joy of Minecraft. To ensure everyone has a great time, we’ve established a few simple guidelines. Please take a moment to familiarize yourself with them.

1. 😊 Be Kind and Respectful—Treat all players with kindness and respect. This is The Great British Bake Off not Hell's Kitchen. Support each other. Help out if someone is stuck, lost, or looking for inspiration.

2. 🧒 Keep It Family-Friendly—Our server is kid-friendly, so please avoid swearing, rude language, or inappropriate topics. Let’s keep our conversations and creations suitable for all ages.

4. 📣 Communication Is Key—Use Discord or the server chat to stay in touch, plan projects, or ask for help. Horizon is a positive and inclusive space for everyone. If you have concerns that someone is not following the guidelines, please reach out to tres@treshenry.net.

5. 🥳 Have Fun!— Explore, create, and enjoy. Minecraft offers endless possibilities, and together, we continue to make Horizon a wonderful place to escape the shenanigans of RL. 💛
