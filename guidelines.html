Horizon Community Guidelines

Welcome to Horizon, a Minecraft survival multiplayer (SMP) server where friends and family come together to explore, create, and have fun!

Our server is a safe place for players of all ages, gender identities, backgrounds, and sexual orientations to gather and share in the joy of Minecraft. To ensure everyone has a great time, we’ve established a few simple guidelines. Please take a moment to familiarize yourself with them.

  1. 😊 Be Kind and Respectful

    Treat all players with kindness and respect. This is The Great British Bake Off not Hell's Kitchen. We support each other. We help each other out when asked.

  2. 🧒 Keep It Family-Friendly

    Our server is kid-friendly. We avoid swearing, rude language, or inappropriate topics. We keep our conversations and creations suitable for all ages.

  3. 📣 Communication Is Key

    We use Discord or server chat to stay in touch, plan projects, or ask for help. Horizon is a positive and inclusive space for everyone. If you’re concerned that someone is not following the guidelines, please email tres@treshenry.net or DM either @ohnoimdead or @eldoranyx on Discord.

  4. 🥳 Have Fun!

    Explore, create, and enjoy! Minecraft offers endless possibilities, and together, we make Horizon a wonderful place to escape the shenanigans of RL. 💛
